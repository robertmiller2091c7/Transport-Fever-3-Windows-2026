## 🕹️ About

Transport Fever 3 is a transport tycoon and economic simulation game developed by Urban Games and published by Paradox Interactive. Build and optimize rail, road, sea, and air networks across more than 125 years of transport history, moving passengers and cargo while growing towns into cities. The game is scheduled for release on September 29, 2026, and is currently available as a pre-release.

Transport enthusiasts, strategy players, and creative builders who enjoy detailed management simulations with long-term planning.

## ✨ Features

- 🚂 **Transport Empire** — Operate trains, buses, trams, trucks, ships, aircraft, and helicopters across evolving eras.
- 🌍 **Connected World** — Link towns, industries, and regions through land, sea, and air routes.
- 📦 **Deep Logistics** — Manage cargo chains, warehouses, specialized terminals, and demand-driven industries.
- 🏙️ **Dynamic Cities** — Grow villages into cities with evolving districts, traffic, demand, and infrastructure needs.
- 💰 **Tycoon Management** — Balance revenue, maintenance, loans, contracts, reputation, noise, and pollution.
- 🗺️ **Multiple Modes** — Play campaign missions, adjustable-difficulty tycoon scenarios, or unrestricted sandbox games.
- 🌦️ **Living Simulation** — Experience dynamic weather, day-night cycles, lighting, and distinct climate zones.
- 🛠️ **Create and Mod** — Design custom maps and expand the game with improved tools and an in-game mod browser.
